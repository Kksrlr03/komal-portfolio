Projects

ProdLoca – Find Local Products Nearby

ProdLoca helps people discover products in nearby neighbourhood shops instead of relying only on big e-commerce websites. Shop owners can update stock from a simple dashboard, while customers search, view each item on a live Mapbox map, and message the shop or place an order — all inside the app.

My goal was to solve a real everyday problem: many local stores have what we need, but we don’t know it. ProdLoca brings their shelves to a phone screen and keeps the money in the community.

Tech used: React, Firebase Realtime DB, Mapbox GL

Quote Terminals – AI Twitter Quote Bot

Quote Terminals is a fully automated system that shares new motivational quotes on my X (Twitter) account every five minutes — without human input. ChatGPT creates the quotes, a quick sentiment check keeps them positive, and a database makes sure no quote repeats.

The pipeline helped me learn how to connect AI text generation with simple online automation tools. It ran smoothly for weeks until I paused it because of IFTTT costs, but I can restart anytime.

Tech used: ChatGPT, Hugging Face sentiment check, SQLite/DynamoDB, IFTTT
