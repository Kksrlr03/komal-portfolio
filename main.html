Projects

ProdLoca – Find Local Products Nearby

ProdLoca helps people discover products in nearby neighbourhood shops instead of relying only on large e-commerce platforms. Shop owners update stock via a simple dashboard; customers search, view items on a live map and message shops directly.

This project solved a local problem: stores often have what people need but lack a simple way to surface their inventory online. ProdLoca bridges that gap and helps local commerce.

Tech used: React, Firebase Realtime DB, Mapbox GL

Quote Terminals – AI Twitter Quote Bot

Quote Terminals is an automated pipeline that posts motivational quotes. Text is generated by an LLM, filtered by sentiment checks, and persisted to avoid repeats. It taught me practical integration of AI with automation tools.

Tech used: OpenAI/ChatGPT, Hugging Face sentiment, small DB, IFTTT integrations.
